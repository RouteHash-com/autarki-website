Wallet

You hold the 24 words.

Autarki does not hold them, and there is no account that can reset them. The same install is the shop and the customer.

The 24 words XBT and BTC Balance Lightning The Autarki LSP Your node

Write them on paper.

  • 24 English words, shown once. They restore the funds on another machine.
  • They do not restore the shop book, the orders, the catalog, or a Lightning channel.
  • A new wallet can be 99 digits, each from 1 through 6. This computer adds its own randomness. The same digits do not rebuild a wallet. The 24 words shown once are the only backup.
  • A password is optional. At least 12 characters. It encrypts only your copy of the wallet file.
  • It is not part of the 24 words. Blank leaves the words unencrypted on your computer. A backup of that service is then the seed.
  • Closing the browser does not lock the page. Restarting Autarki does. There is no Lock button.

XBT and BTC.

Pay with XBT or Pay with BTC. You choose the coin, and the payment stays in that coin.

Blake2b XBT

Buttons say XBT or Pay with XBT. The node is Bitcoin Knots. Lightning is Blake2b Lightning.

SHA-256 BTC

Buttons say BTC or Pay with BTC. The node is Bitcoin Core. Lightning is SHA-256 Lightning.

  • XBT and SHA-256 BTC share history only up to the fork. A payment is signed for one chain.
  • Coins from the wrong chain do not pay the sale.
  • Prices are whole sats. A dollar hint, in US dollars or Canadian dollars, does not change the sats.

Receive, Send, and Balance.

  • Whole sats. Prepare builds it. Sign is separate. Broadcast is separate.
  • The browser never receives the signed transaction.
  • No confirmation: not spendable. An ordinary coin spends after one.
  • A mining reward waits longer. On XBT the wait depends on the block. On BTC it is 100 confirmations.
  • Balance is confirmed coins still held. Lightning is a separate jar, not added to Balance.
  • Moving coins into Lightning sends them out of Balance. They confirm before a channel can open.
  • A chain can be hidden in your browser. The saved connection stays on your computer.

Lightning, on AMD and Intel.

Where it runs.

  • AMD or Intel StartOS, Umbrel, and Ubuntu start Blake2b Lightning and SHA-256 Lightning.
  • A Raspberry Pi and Umbrel arm64 start the wallet and the shop. Lightning stays off.
  • A Lightning payment, a shipment, and Mine need AMD or Intel.

What you skip.

  • No second Lightning app.
  • Do not paste a node address, a macaroon, or a certificate.
  • Your wallet's 24 words are not given to either Lightning node.

First start.

  • A new wallet creates both homes, writes each missing seed, and leaves an existing seed as it is.
  • It starts both programs and saves the local link.
  • No coin node of your own: they can still start against Autarki's servers. Your node is used when you connect it.
  • About ten minutes. The page keeps checking. Later starts are quicker.
  • Before a node can take coins, its backup is shown once. You confirm you wrote it down. It is not shown again.
  • Blake2b: that node's own 24 words. Bitcoin: a one-time paper copy of its two seed files.
  • Do not reuse the wallet words. A Bitcoin payment request is refused on Blake2b.

On-chain is your bank. Keep savings there. Lightning is the money you spend. Load it before you go shopping in person.

  • The first load takes about ten minutes. A later payment on a channel that can cover the amount does not wait.
  • Skip this and on-chain still works. Install does not open a channel.
  • Lightning sats are not Balance, and not the shop's accounted sats.
  • A Lightning sale is on the sales spreadsheet and absent from the coin journal.

Why a Lightning channel is necessary.

On-chain needs no channel. A Lightning payment spends sats already locked in a channel between two nodes.

  • On-chain is the bank. Each payment waits for a confirmation and pays a miner fee. Savings stay there.
  • Capacity is the sats locked in the channel.
  • Outbound capacity is what your node can send. Those coins sit on your side.
  • Inbound capacity is what your node can receive. Those coins sit on the other node's side.
  • You can pay only up to your outbound capacity. A shop can receive only up to its inbound capacity.
  • A channel you just funded has almost no inbound capacity. The coins are on your side, so a customer cannot pay you on it yet.

The Autarki LSP, or your own channel.

The Autarki LSP is a Lightning node you open one channel to. In the wallet that button says Use Autarki's pool.

Compared Autarki LSP Your own channel
Who it pays This shop, or this seller, and later shops that already have inbound capacity with the LSP. Only that one node.
Fee Routing at most 1,000 sats. Your coins stay on your side until the payment. No LSP fee. The wallet already knows the node.
First open About ten minutes, and a miner fee. Nothing opens until you confirm. The same wait, and a miner fee. Nothing opens until you confirm.
Inbound for a shop Bought as room. The seller's node stays on. It does not need its own confirmed sats. A buyer can still open toward you. Zero inbound capacity does not block that buyer.
  • There is an Autarki LSP for Blake2b Lightning, on XBT, and one for SHA-256 Lightning, on BTC.
  • A channel that can already pay is used. No choice appears.
  • If the Autarki LSP cannot be reached, the page offers the direct channel. Nothing was sent.
  • Install does not open a channel. A mining payment does not use the LSP.
  • The LSP does not hold the sale and does not take a cut of the catalog price. Lightning still costs: routing at most 1,000 sats, and room of 1,200 sats for 20,000 or 2,000 sats for 100,000. A buyer still needs a channel.

What the fees are, in dollars.

Sats are the price. One XBT sat and one BTC sat are not the same dollar. These hints follow the NeoEX USDC quote: about $280 for one XBT, and about $75,000 for one BTC. They move. A dollar hint does not change the sats.

Cost Sats XBT hint BTC hint
Routing through the LSP, each payment At most 1,000 About $0.0028 About $0.75
Inbound room of 20,000 sats, once 1,200 About $0.0034 About $0.90
Inbound room of 100,000 sats, once 2,000 About $0.0056 About $1.50
  • On XBT those three fees are each under one cent. On BTC the routing cap is about 75 cents.
  • 20,000 sats of inbound capacity is about $0.06 of XBT, or about $15 of BTC. 100,000 sats is about $0.28 of XBT, or about $75 of BTC. That figure is the capacity.
  • A card takes a percent of every sale. A common online rate is 2.9% plus $0.30, about $3.20 on a $100 sale. In person, 2.6% plus $0.15 is about $2.75 on $100.
  • Autarki does not charge the card and does not take that percent. On a $100 BTC sale the routing cap is about $0.75. On a sale near $5, the online card fee is about $0.45, and the BTC cap can cost more.
  • Room is paid once. Opening a channel also pays a miner fee. That amount is whatever the chain asks that day.
  • A node publishes a fee to forward toward each peer, and each peer publishes a fee to forward back. Those are the fees in both directions. The routing cap is what you pay through the LSP. Inbound capacity is the room a shop buys.

When a payment needs a new channel.

  • A channel that can already pay is used. No choice appears.
  • A store order uses that store's node. A classified uses the seller's node.
  • A mining payment does not use the Autarki LSP.
  • A new channel asks first. Nothing opens until you confirm.
  • You never type the shop's node. Blake2b and SHA-256 already know the Autarki LSP.
  • Both ways use coins already confirmed in that node's wallet, and both pay a miner fee.
  • If the Autarki LSP cannot be reached, the page says so and offers the direct channel. Nothing was sent.

Use Autarki's pool

One channel, later shops.

  • Pays this shop, or this seller, and later shops that already have room.
  • Routing fee at most 1,000 sats. Your coins stay on your side until the payment.
  • First opening waits about ten minutes. A later payment can reuse it.

Open a direct channel

This shop, or this seller.

  • Open a direct channel to this shop, or Open a direct channel to this seller.
  • No LSP fee. It pays only that one node.
  • Same ten minutes. A later payment to that node can reuse it.

A seller does not have to find a peer.

On-chain needs no channel. Lightning offers the same two ways on Blake2b and on SHA-256.

Pay for pool room

The fee fills Send.

  • 20,000 sats of room costs 1,200 sats. 100,000 sats of room costs 2,000 sats.
  • Pay 1,200 sats for Blake2b pool room. Pay 1,200 sats for Bitcoin pool room.
  • Nothing is sent until Prepare, then Broadcast. The page does not open the room.
  • Then a buyer who already has a channel to the Autarki LSP can pay this node. Routing at most 1,000 sats.
  • The seller's node has to be on. It does not need its own confirmed sats to accept that channel.

Use a direct channel

Leave the fee unpaid.

  • A buyer opens a channel to this shop or this seller. It pays only them.
  • Zero incoming room does not block a buyer who will open a channel.
  • A buyer who will not open one needs the room to be there already.
  • A channel the seller just funded has almost no incoming room. Those coins are on the seller's side.
  • The Autarki LSP does not hold the sale and does not take a cut of the catalog price.
  • The room fee and the routing fee are the Lightning costs.
  • A shipped order stays on the seller's node until Mark received, and only after a float. The Autarki LSP does not replace that hold.

How a shipment is protected

Your node, or Autarki's servers.

  • Connect your own coin node under Settings, then Coin Nodes, or use Autarki's servers for ordinary addresses.
  • Until your node is connected, those servers can see a normal address.
  • They do not make a silent-payment code, and they cannot see a coin paid to one.
  • A silent-payment code appears after your own node is connected.
  • XBT Knots and Bitcoin Core do not run on the same computer.
