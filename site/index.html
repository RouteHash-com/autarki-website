What you can do.

You hold the keys.

You keep the 24 words. Autarki does not hold the coins, clear the sale, or take a cut of the catalog price. Lightning still has a routing fee and a room fee.

Those amounts

Lightning is in the app.

Lightning for XBT and for BTC comes with the wallet. You do not install a second app. You can also use Lightning on another Autarki that you keep running. A shop can ask for the other coin. You spend the coin you already hold. The shop is paid in the coin it asked for.

The stall is public.

The order is a private note. The relay that carries it cannot read it. You pay the person, not a platform.

Sell at the counter.

Cash or a card can pay at the counter. The card machine is the clerk's. Autarki records the sale and does not charge the card.

The shop

Ship a classified.

When the seller holds a float, the coins stay on the seller's node until Mark received. Without that float, the shipment is not held. Not received asks the named referee. The referee does not hold the coins. A shop sale does not name one. A store stall does not name one.

How a shipment is protected

Pay with the coin you hold.

Pay with XBT still spends XBT. Pay with BTC still spends BTC. The 1.5% fee applies when you pay the other coin, plus the normal network fees. It does not apply when you pay the coin asked for. If that direction cannot pay, the other coin stays off the screen.

Paying the other coin

You hold the 24 words.

Write them on paper. They restore the funds, not the shop book or a Lightning channel.

The wallet

Hire a rig.

The next reward address is your wallet's. Mine itself does not spend. Lightning leaves when you press Pay to start. A RouteHash balance leaves when you confirm the hours.

Mining

Send a private note.

One private note can go beside a shop name. It does not change the order.

The shop

Four sales, as they happen.

At the counter

  1. The buyer presses Check-Out, then Pay Counter.
  2. The clerk opens the ticket and presses Cash, or a card.
  3. The clerk presses Mark this sale paid.

A shipped classified

  1. The buyer presses Ship and pays with Lightning.
  2. The seller marks it shipped and enters the carrier and the tracking number.
  3. The buyer presses Mark received. When the seller holds a float, the seller is paid.

Without that float, the shipment is not held.

How the coins wait

A private note

  1. Press Message beside a shop name.
  2. Write one note and press Send.

A listed rig

  1. Paste a RouteHash token and press Connect RouteHash.
  2. Press Mine XBT.

Install Autarki.

It runs on your computer. A 64-bit Android phone can join Google Play open testing, or install the file. An iPhone can join the public TestFlight test. A phone on the same network can still open your computer's page.
