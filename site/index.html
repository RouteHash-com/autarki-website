No platform.Just the sale.

Autarki is your wallet and your shop. You hold the keys, you pay over Lightning or on-chain, and you buy and sell without a platform in the middle.

Look through the notice board Install Autarki

You hold the keys.

You keep the 24 words. Autarki does not hold the coins, does not clear the sale, and does not take a cut of the catalog price. A buyer may pay a routing fee of at most 1,000 sats. A seller may pay a room fee: 20,000 sats of room costs 1,200 sats, and 100,000 sats of room costs 2,000 sats. Those are Lightning costs. The pool does not hold the sale. A shipped order stays on the seller's node until Mark received, and only after a float.

Lightning is in the app.

On AMD and Intel, StartOS, Umbrel, and Ubuntu start Blake2b Lightning and SHA-256 Lightning. You do not install a second Lightning app. A Raspberry Pi and Umbrel arm64 leave Lightning off.

Two people settle it.

The stall is public. The order is private. The node that carries the order cannot read it. You pay the person, not a platform.

Mining

Four sales, as they happen.

At the counter

A clerk marks a sale paid with cash. The card machine is the clerk's. Autarki records the sale. The page does not charge the card.

A shipped classified

A seller posts a classified. The buyer chooses Ship. The coins stay held until Mark received. Not received asks the named referee. The referee does not hold the coins.

How the coins wait

A private note

Someone taps Message beside a shop name, writes one note, and sends it. The order on the screen does not change. An older install does not show Message until it is updated.

A listed rig

Someone pastes a RouteHash token and clicks Mine XBT. The next reward address is already this wallet's. Nothing is spent until that click, or until the hours are confirmed.
