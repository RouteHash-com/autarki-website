No platform.Just the sale.

Autarki is your wallet and your shop. You hold the keys, you pay over Lightning or on-chain, and you buy and sell without a platform in the middle.

Install Autarki Look through a shop

Autarki is a beta. It runs on your own computer: StartOS, Umbrel, or Ubuntu. A phone on the same network opens that computer's page. There is no separate phone app.

You hold the keys.

You keep the 24 words. Autarki does not hold the coins, does not clear the sale, and does not take a fee from it.

Lightning is in the app.

On an AMD or Intel computer, Autarki starts Blake2b Lightning and SHA-256 Lightning. You do not install a second Lightning app. A Raspberry Pi runs the wallet and the shop, and leaves Lightning off.

Two people settle it.

The stall is public. The order is private. The node that carries the order cannot read it. You pay the person, not a platform.

What you can do.

You hold the 24 words.

Write them on paper. They restore the funds on another machine. They do not restore the shop book, the orders, or a Lightning channel. Leave the password blank and the words are stored unencrypted on this device.

The wallet

Three coins.

Pay with XBT, Pay with BTC, or Pay with XBTC. Coins from the wrong chain do not pay the sale. A Bitcoin payment to an XBTC address does not arrive as XBTC.

Coins and Lightning

Lightning stays in the app.

An AMD or Intel computer starts Lightning. A Raspberry Pi and Umbrel arm64 leave it off. XBTC has no Lightning and is not offered for a shipment.

Where Lightning runs

The stall is public.

You can look before you make a wallet. The order is a private note, and the relay cannot read it. Pay Counter records cash or a card. This page does not charge the card. Ordinary addresses use Autarki's servers until your own node is connected. A silent-payment code appears after that.

The shop

Ship it.

The buyer pays with Lightning. The coins sit on the seller's node until the parcel is marked received. Cancel stays open until it is marked shipped. A named referee does not hold the coins.

Classifieds

Hire a rig.

Mining hires a listed rig from RouteHash. Nothing is spent until you click Mine, or until the hours are confirmed. XBTC is not a mining card.

Mining

Four sales, as they happen.

At the counter

A clerk marks a sale paid with cash. The card machine is the clerk's. Autarki records the sale. The page does not charge the card.

A shipped classified

A seller posts a classified. The buyer chooses Ship. The coins stay held until Mark received. Not received asks the named referee. The referee does not hold the coins.

A private note

Someone taps Message beside a shop name, writes one note, and sends it. The order on the screen does not change. An older install does not show Message until it is updated.

A listed rig

Someone pastes a RouteHash token and clicks Mine XBT. The next reward address is already this wallet's. Nothing is spent until that click, or until the hours are confirmed.
