# Autarki

> The buyer pays you, and you keep the keys. Autarki is the wallet and the shop.

The page is [https://autarki.app/](https://autarki.app/).

## No platform. Just the sale.

The buyer pays you, and you keep the keys.

- You keep the 24 words. Autarki does not hold the coins, clear the sale, or take a cut of the catalog price. Lightning still has a routing fee and a room fee. The amounts are on the [wallet page](https://autarki.app/wallet#fee-spot).
- Lightning for XBT and for BTC comes with the wallet. You do not install a second app. You can also use Lightning on another Autarki that you keep running. A shop can ask for the other coin. You spend the coin you already hold. The shop is paid in the coin it asked for.
- The stall is public. The order is a private note. The relay that carries it cannot read it. You pay the person, not a platform.
- A clerk marks a sale paid with cash or a card. The card machine is the clerk's. Autarki records the sale and does not charge the card.
- The buyer presses Ship. With a float, the coins stay on the seller's node until Mark received. Without that float, the shipment is not held. Not received asks the named referee. The referee does not hold the coins. A shop sale does not name one. A store stall does not name one.
- Pay with XBT still spends XBT. Pay with BTC still spends BTC. The fee is 1.5%, plus the normal network fees. If that direction cannot pay, the other coin stays off the screen.
- Write the 24 words on paper. They restore the funds, not the shop book or a Lightning channel.
- Paste a RouteHash token, then press Mine XBT or Mine BTC. The next reward address is your wallet's. Nothing is spent until that press, or until the hours are confirmed.
- Someone taps Message beside a shop name. The note does not change the order.
- It runs on your computer. A 64-bit Android phone can join Google Play open testing, or install the file. An iPhone can join the public TestFlight test. A phone on the same network can still open your computer's page.
