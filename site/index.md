# Autarki

> The buyer pays you, and you keep the keys. Autarki is the wallet and the shop.

The page is [https://autarki.app/](https://autarki.app/).

## No platform. Just the sale.

The buyer pays you, and you keep the keys.

- You keep the 24 words.
- Autarki does not hold the coins, clear the sale, or take a cut of the catalog price.
- Lightning still costs: routing at most 1,000 sats. Room is 1,200 sats for 20,000, or 2,000 sats for 100,000.
- The pool does not hold the sale. A shipped order stays on the seller's node until Mark received, and only after a float.
- Lightning starts with the wallet. You do not install a second app.
- The stall is public. The order is private. You pay the person, not a platform.
- Pay with XBT or Pay with BTC. You choose the coin, and the payment stays in that coin.
- It runs on your computer. A phone on the same network opens that page. There is no separate phone app.
