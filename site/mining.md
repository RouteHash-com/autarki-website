# Mining

> Paste a RouteHash token, then click Mine XBT or Mine BTC. The next reward address is your wallet's. Nothing is spent until Mine.

The page is [https://autarki.app/mining](https://autarki.app/mining).

## Hire a rig. Rewards come back to your wallet.

The hire happens in your wallet. routehash.com is where the rig is listed. Your computer does not mine.

- Open a RouteHash account at [routehash.com](https://routehash.com).
- Paste that account's API token. The same connection is under Settings, then Other.
- Mine XBT hires a listed Blake2b rig and pays in Blake2b XBT. Mine BTC hires a listed SHA-256 rig and pays in SHA-256 BTC.
- The next reward address is already your wallet's. Nothing is spent until Mine, or until the hours are confirmed.
- A mining payment does not use the Autarki LSP.
- Mine needs AMD or Intel. It stays off on a Raspberry Pi and on Umbrel arm64.
- Rewards come back to your wallet.
- After RouteHash is connected, Balance lists Open Mining Invoices.
- A reward becomes spendable on Balance after that chain's wait. On XBT the wait is longer and depends on the block. On BTC it is 100 confirmations.
- The Mining card is not a second balance.
- A 64-bit Android phone can install its own copy. Mining still needs AMD or Intel.

See [Install](https://autarki.app/install.md).
