# Mining

> Paste a RouteHash token, then press Mine XBT or Mine BTC. The next reward address is your wallet's. Mine itself does not spend. RouteHash is a separate site. Autarki connects to it and does not collect the hire.

The page is [https://autarki.app/mining](https://autarki.app/mining).

## Hire a rig. Rewards come back to your wallet.

The hire happens in your wallet. routehash.com is where the rig is listed. RouteHash is a separate site. Autarki connects to it and does not collect the hire. Your computer does not mine. Mine needs AMD or Intel. It stays off on a Raspberry Pi and on Umbrel arm64.

- Open a RouteHash account at [routehash.com](https://routehash.com).
- Paste that account's API token and press Connect RouteHash. The same connection is under Settings, then Other.
- Press Mine XBT for a listed Blake2b rig. That hire pays in Blake2b XBT. Press Mine BTC for a listed SHA-256 rig. That hire pays in SHA-256 BTC.
- The next reward address is already your wallet's. Mine itself does not spend.
- Hire by the hour from a RouteHash balance. That path does not spend your wallet's Lightning. Nothing is spent until you confirm those hours. A short account says to deposit on RouteHash.
- Or pay the meter from Lightning in your wallet, for the coin you are mining, when your wallet can cover it. Press Pay to start, or Pay to keep.
- A mining payment does not use Autarki's pool. Autarki does not collect the hire.
- Rewards come back to your wallet.
- Before RouteHash is connected, Balance says to hire a rig and that rewards come back to your wallet.
- After it is connected, Balance lists Open Mining Invoices for the hires still open.
- A reward becomes spendable on Balance after that chain's wait. On XBT the wait is longer and depends on the block. On BTC it is 100 confirmations.
- The Mining card is not a second balance.
- A 64-bit Android phone can install its own copy. Mining still needs AMD or Intel.

See [Install](https://autarki.app/install.md).
