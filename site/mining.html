Mining

Hire a rig. Rewards come back here.

The hire happens in this wallet. routehash.com is where the rig is listed. This computer does not mine.

The hire, in order.

  1. Open a RouteHash account at routehash.com.
  2. Paste that account's API token. The same connection is under Settings, then Other.
  3. Mine XBT hires a listed Blake2b rig and pays in Blake2b XBT. Mine BTC hires a listed SHA-256 rig and pays in SHA-256 BTC. XBTC is not a mining card.
  4. The next reward address is already this wallet's. Nothing is spent until Mine, or until the hours are confirmed.

What leaves this wallet.

  • A rig can be hired by the hour from a RouteHash balance. That path does not spend this wallet's Lightning.
  • A short account says to deposit on RouteHash.
  • The meter is paid from this wallet's Lightning node for that chain, when a channel can cover it.
  • A mining payment does not use Autarki's pool.
  • Mine needs AMD or Intel. It stays off on a Raspberry Pi and on Umbrel arm64.
  • Rewards come back to this wallet.

Where the coins show.

  • Before RouteHash is connected, Balance says to hire a rig and that rewards come back here.
  • After it is connected, Balance lists Open Mining Invoices.
  • A reward becomes spendable on Balance after that chain's wait: 100 confirmations on BTC. On XBT the wait is longer and depends on the block.
  • The Mining card is not a second balance.
