# Support. Autarki

> Help with Autarki is in Discord #autarki. This site does not sign in and cannot recover the 24 words.

This is the support page for the program and for this site. This page is [https://autarki.app/support](https://autarki.app/support).

## Where to write

Bug reports go to the RouteHash Discord channel [#autarki](https://discord.gg/VBxvzQXvt).

Write what you saw, the page you were on, and the kind of computer or phone. Do not send the 24 words, a wallet file, a seed file, or a payment code.

## What stays with you

This site does not sign in. Autarki cannot reset a wallet and cannot recover the 24 words. They stay on your device.

Install steps are on the [install page](https://autarki.app/install.md). The privacy policy is on the [privacy page](https://autarki.app/privacy.md).

The program is in beta.
