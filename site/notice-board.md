# Notice board

> A wallet publishes the stall. This page reads the relay, stores nothing, and does not take an order.

The page is [https://autarki.app/notice-board](https://autarki.app/notice-board).

## Look through what is published.

A person publishes a [Store](https://autarki.app/notice-board#stores) or a [Classified](https://autarki.app/notice-board#classifieds) from their own wallet. This page reads the relay. It keeps no copy and takes no order.

- The stall and the classified are public notes. The wallet publishes them.
- A store shows Lightning on XBT or Lightning on BTC when its published stall includes incoming room. A classified from that seller shows the same line. This page does not see the node.
- This page asks one relay, shows what came back, and closes. Nothing is stored on this site.
- A note this board does not carry is left off. A report does not remove the ad. Block and Ignore hide it on that device only. The rules are on the [board rules](https://autarki.app/board.md) page. The relay can still keep the original note.
- A description that starts with Available only on Autarki; is left off, so that note does not sit beside the sale.
- Search a shop, a product, a place, or a public name. Search and the chips run on this page. Nothing is ranked by payment.
- Autarki does not approve a stall and does not take the order.
- The order is a private note. The relay cannot read it.
- A shop uses this relay until it names its own. This page does not follow a shop onto another relay.
- You can look before you make a wallet. Sending an order needs the wallet on your computer.
- Looking is possible here. An order needs the wallet on your computer, or on a [64-bit Android phone](https://autarki.app/install.md).

The stores section and the classifieds section are filled in the browser from what the relay returns. This file does not list those shops. The Classifieds heading links to [Post an ad](https://autarki.app/classifieds#publish).
