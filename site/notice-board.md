# Notice board

> A wallet publishes the stall. This page reads the relay, stores nothing, and does not take an order.

The page is [https://autarki.app/notice-board](https://autarki.app/notice-board).

## Look through what is published.

A person publishes a stall or a classified from their own wallet. This page reads the relay. It keeps no copy and takes no order.

- The stall and the classified are public notes. The wallet publishes them.
- This page asks one relay, shows what came back, and closes. Nothing is stored on this site.
- Search a shop, a product, a place, or a public name. Search and the chips run on this page. Nothing is ranked by payment.
- Autarki does not approve a stall and does not take the order.
- The order is a private note. The relay cannot read it.
- A shop uses this relay until it names its own. This page does not follow a shop onto another relay.
- You can look before you make a wallet. Sending an order needs the wallet on your computer.
- Looking is possible here. There is no separate phone app.

The stores section and the classifieds section are filled in the browser from what the relay returns. This file does not list those shops.
