# Classifieds

> Ship pays with Lightning. With a float, the coins sit on the seller's node until Mark received. The named referee decides and does not hold the coins.

The page is [https://autarki.app/classifieds](https://autarki.app/classifieds).

## Ship it. The coins wait.

Ship pays with Lightning. With a float, the coins sit on the seller's node until Mark received. Autarki does not hold them.

- A separate public board from the shop catalog. Off until Enable Classifieds under Settings. A new wallet leaves them off.
- Goods, services, rentals, jobs, giveaways, and personals. Same key as the shop. No store required.
- The buyer chooses Pick up or Ship. There is no default.
- Pick up pays on-chain or with Lightning, in XBT or in BTC. Pick up does not wait.
- Ship pays with Lightning only. Pick up can still pay on-chain. On-chain has no hold, and a referee cannot move those coins.
- Lightning for a shipment is in the AMD and Intel StartOS package, the Ubuntu image, the Umbrel amd64 image file, and Android. The Umbrel community store, Raspberry Pi, Umbrel arm64, and the iPhone build do not start Lightning, so they do not pay a shipment.
- The coins sit on the seller's Lightning node. They are not in Autarki's hands. The seller's wallet has to stay on through the window. A phone that only opens the page does not hold the coins.
- A shipped order is not held until a float is set aside. Provide a float records the choice. It does not send coins.
- A referee is optional. Payment waits until both sides name the same person, or both name nobody. That person is told when the names match. The referee does not hold the coins. There is no refund button. A return is this hold sending the coins back.
- The ad names two dates. The seller has one to four days to mark it shipped, with a carrier and a tracking number. The usual hold after that is 14 days. An overseas ad can say 28 days. The ad shows which one it is.
- Before it is shipped, Cancel is still open and the coins go back. After it is shipped, Cancel is closed. Mark received pays the seller. If the buyer does nothing, the seller is paid when the hold ends, only while that wallet is on. If the wallet stays off through the window, the coins go back.
- Nora buys a shipped order from Eli. She pays with Lightning. The coins sit on Eli's node. Eli marks it shipped before the ship-by day. Nora presses Mark received. Eli is paid.
- Nora presses Not received. She can leave a short note. Eli can leave one too. Not received stops both automatic moves. The coins stay where they are.
- A decision for Eli pays Eli. A decision for Nora sends the coins back. The order stays on the books as returned, not cancelled. If no decision arrives, the window still sends the coins back to Nora. If Eli's wallet stays off until the window ends, a decision to pay him does not finish, and the coins return on their own. Mark received closes once a decision is stored.
- Check the carrier saves the latest scan as words. Canada Post, UPS, FedEx, and DHL are the names it can look up. The person does not create a carrier account. The scan does not pay anyone, and it does not send the coins back.
- Four public signs. None is required to buy or sell. Nobody types a count. No star.
- A 64-bit Android phone can install its own copy. A phone on the same network can still open your computer's page.

## Post an ad

A signed note from any Nostr key shows on the wallet board when the note is one this board carries. The poster does not need an Autarki wallet. A Nostr extension in the browser signs the note. This page does not ask for a secret key. The note is published to the relay the notice board reads. A note sent only to some other relay never arrives. Classifieds has to be turned on, and the wallet has to be unlocked, or that board stays hidden. The rules are on the [board rules](https://autarki.app/board.md) page.

- Choose one category first. Offer and Hire are shown only for a job. A hire carries no payment code.
- The title is one line of at most 80 characters. A missing title is dropped.
- A listing id is 1 to 256 characters with no spaces. The same key and the same id replace the note.
- The description is at most 4000 characters.
- Country, province, city, and neighbourhood are each one line, at most 40 characters. Use my location asks the browser. The position is not saved and is not sent to Autarki. A refused prompt stops there.
- The price is a whole number of sats on XBT or BTC. Empty or zero is not payable. A priced ad with no silent-payment code still appears, and the buyer is told it cannot be paid yet. This page cannot make the code. XBT and BTC each get a silent-payment code, so a buyer can pay either chain.
- A dollar amount, a fraction, or any other currency is shown as text and cannot be paid.
- Goods, a service, or a rental with a price above zero names 1 to 4 days, and Domestic or Overseas. A job, a giveaway, a personal, a hire, and a zero price publish no ship tags.
- An image is one https address on a line. Four is the limit. Images are for goods, a service, a rental, or a giveaway.
- A map link is the https address from OpenStreetMap, Google Maps, or Apple Maps. A city name is not a map link. A map is for goods, a service, or a rental.
- Ship appears when the note names a public Lightning node for a chain that has a code.
- Mark sold or Cancel this ad hides the note. A person a wallet has blocked or ignored is hidden on that wallet only.
- This page checks the note before it is signed. A note this board does not carry is not sent. A report is sent to the Autarki relay. The ad stays on the board. The relay can still keep the original note.
- Preview this ad shows the note on this page. It does not publish it.
- Show this ad on other markets posts a second note. That note has no price. The sale stays on the relay this board reads. Turning it off and posting drops that second note. Mark sold and Cancel this ad leave it alone.
- Accept the board terms before the first note is saved.
- This site does not keep the key or the note. Use the Autarki app for easy ad management.

Published classifieds are on the [notice board](https://autarki.app/notice-board.md).
