# Shop

> The shop runs on your computer. The stall is public. The order is private. Autarki does not take a cut of the catalog price. A routing fee and a room fee are charged by nodes other people operate. Autarki does not collect them.

The page is [https://autarki.app/shop](https://autarki.app/shop).

## The stall is public. The order is private.

The shop runs on your computer. People can look before they make a wallet. An order needs that wallet, and the relay cannot read it.

- Autarki does not take a cut of the catalog price. A routing fee and a room fee are charged by nodes other people operate. Autarki does not collect them. The amounts are on the [wallet page](https://autarki.app/wallet#fee-spot).
- One product can keep a price in US dollars or Canadian dollars. The dollar price stays and the coin amount is set when you pay. A product still priced in sats stays in sats. A cart can hold both.
- Public pool does not hold the sale. Autarki cannot move those coins. A shop sale does not name a referee. A store stall does not name one. A shipped classified is on the [classifieds page](https://autarki.app/classifieds#protection).
- The stall, each product, and the place are public. The key and the books stay on your computer.
- A shop is the counter until the owner picks Online and publishes. Pay Counter records the sale. It does not charge the card, and it does not talk to a card terminal. Pay Counter records cash, debit, credit, a coupon, store credit, bitcoin, or a mix. A dollar price records those dollars. The page does not write a coin. The clerk uses the card machine.
- On Sales, a dollar row shows the locked dollars and the sats collected beside them. Download sales keeps the currency, the dollar total, and the prices used for that sale.
- Pay with XBT or Pay with BTC. Lightning for XBT and for BTC comes with the wallet. It sits under the coin you pressed.
- The shelf count stays on your computer. It is not published. Clerks are staff names on your computer. They are not published.
- Shop books can be saved and brought back. Save a copy before you replace the computer or wipe the app data. Bring a copy back, then publish. That file leaves out the 24 words, the wallet file, secret keys, and Lightning channel state.
- A paid sale cannot be cancelled. There is no refund button. A refund is a later order.
- Message opens one private note. Send does not create an order, move coins, or change a shipment.
- An older install does not show Message until it is updated.
- A 64-bit Android phone can install its own copy. A phone on the same network can still open your computer's page.

See the published stalls on the [notice board](https://autarki.app/notice-board.md). A shipment is described on [Classifieds](https://autarki.app/classifieds.md).
