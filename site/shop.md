# Shop

> The shop runs on your computer. The stall is public. The order is private. Autarki does not take a cut of the catalog price. Lightning has a routing fee and a room fee.

The page is [https://autarki.app/shop](https://autarki.app/shop).

## The stall is public. The order is private.

The shop runs on your computer. People can look before they make a wallet. An order needs that wallet, and the relay cannot read it.

- Autarki does not take a cut of the catalog price. Lightning still costs: routing at most 1,000 sats, and room of 1,200 sats for 20,000 or 2,000 sats for 100,000.
- The Autarki LSP does not hold the sale. A shipped order stays on the seller's node until Mark received, and only after a float.
- The stall, each product, and the place are public. The key and the books stay on your computer.
- Pay Counter records cash, debit, credit, a coupon, store credit, bitcoin, or a mix. The page does not charge the card or talk to a card terminal. The clerk uses the card machine.
- Pay with XBT or Pay with BTC. Lightning sits under the chain that was pressed. Blake2b Lightning for XBT. SHA-256 Lightning for BTC.
- The shelf count stays on your computer. It is not published. Clerks are staff names on your computer. They are not published.
- A paid sale cannot be cancelled. There is no refund button. A refund is a later order.
- Message opens one private note. Send does not create an order, move coins, or change a shipment.
- An older install does not show Message until it is updated.
- There is no separate phone app.

See the published stalls on the [notice board](https://autarki.app/notice-board.md). A shipment is described on [Classifieds](https://autarki.app/classifieds.md).
